## Contra — English

### Title

> ONE PIXEL: Rebuilding a narrative RPG slice around player comprehension

### Body

> I have published a new production update for **ONE PIXEL**, an iPhone-first cosmic narrative RPG in development at ELVN Studio.
>
> The current vertical slice, **Dock Nine**, begins with an impossible fact: Olan Marr is standing at his own funeral. The player follows a warm child’s cup, two incompatible records and a missing voice into Extra Street—a route that Correction is actively deleting. The slice ends with three legible choices, each saving something and costing something different.
>
> Owner testing exposed three P0 failures: first-time intent was unclear, Act II characters appeared detached from the floor, and the short chiptune loop became irritating. I treated that evidence as a product failure even though the build and CI were green.
>
> The rescue pass introduced an obituary-first opening, escalating diegetic guidance, a dedicated five-character facial animation atlas, an authored narrow walk plane, faster stride-synchronized movement, animation-frame foot contacts, acceleration-based party following, named human anchors, visible failure-forward loss, and seven narrative 8-bit arrangements with independent master/music/SFX controls.
>
> The updated page includes a real Simulator gameplay frame, current character art, an honest development log and a gated roadmap. No release date or commercial-demo PASS is being claimed; uncoached human and full physical-iPhone validation remain the next gate.
>
