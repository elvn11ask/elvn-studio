## Contra

### Title

> ONE PIXEL — Designing a cosmic RPG around memory, mercy and contradiction

### Project/news body

> I have published the first official development page for **ONE PIXEL**, an original, story-driven cosmic RPG in development at ELVN Studio.
>
> The premise is simple: someone was saved, and someone else was forgotten. The universe can revise its own history, while the player and their companions live with the evidence left behind.
>
> The design challenge is to make that premise playable rather than leave it as lore. The locked gameplay model follows a compact loop: **explore → Witness → discover → talk → commit a party plan → act → consequence → party reaction → world change**. Witness reveals contradictions hidden by ordinary history. Commit Time lets the player choose a companion’s contextual plan while the world remains alive. Success, success with a cost, and failure-forward all continue the scene differently.
>
> Current status:
>
> - story: locked;
> - localization architecture: complete;
> - gameplay model: locked for prototyping;
> - visual development: in progress;
> - Relay Six prototype: builds and runs in Simulator;
> - physical iPhone feel validation: still pending.
>
> The development page combines the project’s visual language, spoiler-safe character and world concepts, an accessible Witness-inspired interaction, and an honest milestone record. It is not a release announcement and does not present concept art as finished gameplay.
>
> View the project: https://studio.elvn.monster/one-pixel
