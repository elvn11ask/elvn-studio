## Technical architecture post

Manufacturer Intelligence uses a controlled six-stage flow:

1. Approve the manufacturer source policy.
2. Resolve the tenant, manufacturer and complete orderable MPN.
3. Collect only from approved sources.
4. Store candidate fields with evidence and conflicts.
5. Route candidates through human review.
6. Simulate and approve a reversible publication batch.

The catalog, PIM, ERP or commerce platform remains the system of record. A connector or model never writes directly to the public catalog.

The safer reusable unit is a governed manufacturer connector applied to a bounded product cohort. Every candidate still resolves to one exact manufacturer + complete MPN identity.

## Exact-MPN safety post

Normalized part numbers are useful for search—not authority.

Package, carrier, grade, temperature, voltage and ordering suffixes can identify different products. Manufacturer Intelligence never silently merges variants and never promotes family-level data into an exact orderable product without evidence.

## AI versus verified fact

In Manufacturer Intelligence, AI output and verified evidence are different classes.

AI may help rank sources, classify documents, interpret tables, normalize units or summarize conflicts. It may not verify a fact, infer compliance, merge variants, select a supplier or publish technical data.

The deterministic workflow operates with AI disabled.
