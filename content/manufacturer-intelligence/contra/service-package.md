## 1. Manufacturer Data Assessment

**Short summary:** Audit manufacturer sources, exact-MPN risks and catalog gaps before investing in enrichment automation.

**Detailed description:** A focused discovery engagement for industrial distributors and electronic-component platforms. ELVN Studio maps approved manufacturer sources, ordering-code risks, current product schema, review ownership and connector feasibility, then defines the smallest safe pilot.

**Ideal client:** A B2B catalog owner with repeated manufacturer research, inconsistent provenance or an upcoming PIM/catalog modernization.

**Scope:** Selected manufacturers and representative SKU cohorts; current systems; source rights; identity rules; evidence fields; review and publication boundary.

**Deliverables:** Source-policy matrix, identity-risk register, schema gap analysis, connector feasibility map, prioritized pilot cohort, implementation roadmap and executive readout.

**Timeline:** 2–4 weeks.

**Typical pricing range:** $5,000–$15,000 one-time.

**Requirements:** Business owner, technical owner, representative catalog export, approved source list and access to relevant process documentation.

**Exclusions:** Production connectors, bulk enrichment, legal opinion on source rights, automatic publication and private credentials in public tools.

**Milestones:** Kickoff and data boundary; source/identity audit; findings review; final roadmap.

**Acceptance criteria:** Agreed sources and risks documented; representative identities reviewed; pilot scope, ownership and measurable gates approved.

**Technologies:** Product Data, Data Engineering, PIM, APIs, technical SEO, evidence modeling.

**CTA:** Request a Manufacturer Data Assessment: https://studio.elvn.monster/revenueos/implementation#assessment

## 2. Manufacturer Connector Pilot

**Short summary:** Prove governed manufacturer connectors on a bounded product cohort without allowing direct catalog writes.

**Detailed description:** Design and validate reusable connector patterns for selected manufacturers. The pilot preserves exact orderable MPN identity, captures field evidence and conflicts, and ends with a simulated publication batch.

**Ideal client:** Industrial or component catalog teams that completed source discovery and can supply review ownership.

**Scope:** Typically three to five manufacturers, bounded SKU cohorts, approved sources, deterministic identity, extraction, reviewer workflow and publication simulation.

**Deliverables:** Connector specifications, source policies, evidence candidates, review queue, conflict report, simulation export, runbook and next-phase plan.

**Timeline:** 4–8 weeks after access and decisions.

**Typical pricing range:** $15,000–$40,000 one-time.

**Requirements:** Approved source permissions, catalog/PIM sample, exact-MPN rules, reviewer availability and test environment.

**Exclusions:** Universal manufacturer coverage, unattended verified publication, bypassing access controls, production rollout outside the agreed cohort.

**Milestones:** Source-policy approval; connector prototype; evidence review; publication simulation; acceptance and handover.

**Acceptance criteria:** Exact identities reconcile; unknown policies fail closed; candidate fields retain evidence; conflicts stay review-only; simulation is auditable and reversible.

**Technologies:** API Integration, Data Engineering, TypeScript/Python where appropriate, document extraction, queues and PIM adapters.

**CTA:** Discuss a bounded connector pilot: https://studio.elvn.monster/revenueos/manufacturer-intelligence

**Ideal client:** Catalog, engineering or data teams spending significant time copying technical values from PDFs and tables.

**Scope:** Selected document families and fields, extraction rules, unit normalization, evidence storage, conflict handling and reviewer interface integration.

**Deliverables:** Document taxonomy, field mapping, extraction pipeline, evidence passport output, review workflow, quality report and operating guide.

**Timeline:** 4–10 weeks.

**Typical pricing range:** $20,000–$75,000 one-time.

**Requirements:** Approved documents, target schema, field definitions, representative edge cases and subject-matter reviewers.

**Milestones:** Document audit; extraction prototype; field validation; reviewer integration; acceptance.

**Acceptance criteria:** Agreed fields trace to document locations; qualifiers survive normalization; uncertain/conflicting results remain visibly unverified; acceptance sample passes agreed review criteria.

**Technologies:** Document AI, PDF/table extraction, Data Engineering, AI Integration, PIM and API Integration.

**Ideal client:** A distributor with meaningful catalog gaps and an established data owner who needs auditability rather than bulk generated copy.

**Scope:** Prioritized manufacturers and fields, identity reconciliation, evidence storage, review roles, integration adapter, simulated and controlled publication.

**Deliverables:** Enrichment backlog, evidence candidates, reviewer workspace/configuration, integration contract, publication batches, audit trail and support runbook.

**Typical pricing range:** $40,000–$150,000 one-time, plus separately scoped hosting/support where required.

**Milestones:** Data contract; bounded enrichment run; reviewer acceptance; integration rehearsal; controlled launch.

**Acceptance criteria:** No unresolved identity conflict publishes; each approved field has provenance; publication is reconciled and reversible; agreed legacy routes and catalog behavior remain stable.
