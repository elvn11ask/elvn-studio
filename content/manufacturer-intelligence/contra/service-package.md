# Manufacturer Intelligence Control Center

An invitation-only customer workspace for evidence-based industrial product-data discovery, review and controlled publication.

## The problem

Industrial catalog enrichment fails when similar part numbers are treated as interchangeable, source rights are implicit, AI suggestions become facts or connector output writes directly to public products.

## The system

ELVN Control Center connects read-only catalog data, validates exact manufacturer and MPN identity, launches bounded scans, records field-level evidence, separates conflicts and AI inference, requires human review and simulates publication with zero public writes.

## Services

- Manufacturer Data Assessment
- Product Data Scan Pilot
- Custom Manufacturer Connectors
- Datasheet Intelligence
- Private AI Integration
- Evidence-Based Catalog Enrichment
- RevenueOS Deployment

## Delivery

Assessment → bounded pilot → implementation → annual platform and support. Private Beta access is contract-managed. Customer credentials, source locations and catalog data remain private.
