## Seven-post architecture thread

### 1/7

Industrial catalog enrichment should not begin with “let AI fill the fields.” It should begin with identity, source rights and evidence. Here is the architecture behind ELVN Manufacturer Intelligence v0.1.0-preview. 🧵

#ProductData #IndustrialTech

### 2/7

The identity contract is deterministic: tenant + manufacturer + complete orderable MPN. Normalized codes may help discovery, but they cannot collapse package, carrier, grade or suffix variants.

### 3/7

Each manufacturer connector has an explicit source policy. Unknown access or usage rights fail closed. The connector resolves only approved pages, documents, images and package evidence for a bounded cohort.

### 4/7

Candidate fields keep an evidence passport: raw and normalized value, qualifier, approved source reference, document location, extraction method, confidence, conflict and review state.

### 5/7

AI is optional. A customer-approved model can assist discovery, classification, extraction and reviewer summaries. It cannot verify a fact, merge variants, infer compliance or publish.

### 6/7

Publication is a separate controlled batch. Teams can simulate, approve, audit and reverse changes. The existing catalog, PIM, ERP or commerce platform remains authoritative.

### 7/7

The preview is available through selected private implementation engagements, beginning with a Manufacturer Data Assessment and a bounded connector pilot.
