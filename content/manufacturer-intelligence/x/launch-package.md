## Technical thread

1/ Industrial catalogs contain orderable variants, not similar strings. Exact manufacturer + MPN is the identity boundary.

2/ A source policy decides what can be automated, stored or linked before a connector runs.

3/ Scans are bounded. Quotas, runtime and concurrency are explicit inputs, not afterthoughts.

4/ Observed evidence, AI inference, unknowns and conflicts remain different fact classes.

5/ Review happens at field level. Publication simulation performs zero public writes.

6/ Two-person approval and canary policy protect the catalog. Pilot adapters are read-only.

7/ AI is optional. The deterministic workflow remains complete without it.
