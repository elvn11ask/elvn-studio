## Business context

Hospitality visitors make decisions visually, but they also need precise information about rooms, location, communication, and booking. On mobile, every extra step makes the direct channel less useful.

## Challenge

The experience had to balance atmospheric presentation with practical clarity and integrate external booking and map services without letting those services dominate the page.

## Solution

I created a responsive content hierarchy that moves from the property story to individual accommodation details and a clear booking action. Maps and communication options appear where they answer an immediate visitor question.

## Engineering decisions

Responsive image delivery, semantic page structure, explicit third-party boundaries, and resilient link behavior keep the site lightweight and understandable. HTTPS and deployment validation are part of the release rather than an afterthought.

## Validation

The direct journey is reviewed on mobile and desktop, including external handoffs, contact actions, page metadata, and common failure paths. No booking or revenue uplift is claimed without access to reliable commercial evidence.

## Current state

PeterhofApart operates as a public hospitality website and direct booking entry point.
