## Thread

1. Industrial revenue evidence lives across catalogs, inboxes, CRM, ERP, sourcing files, quotes, and finance. A dashboard can aggregate it; it rarely explains the chain.

2. Revenue Knowledge Graph is a projection, not another hidden system of record. It is rebuilt from append-only, versioned events.

3. Every edge records where it came from. Manufacturer + exact MPN defines product identity. Financial facts require currency and observed time.

4. AI is optional, read-only, and off by default. Recommendations stay separate from facts and require human review before commercial action.

5. We are opening selected Design Partner implementations. Start with one revenue path, shadow it, reconcile it, then expand.
