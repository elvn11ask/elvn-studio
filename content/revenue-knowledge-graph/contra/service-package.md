# Contra service package

## Project title

Revenue Knowledge Graph for Industrial B2B Sales

## Project post

Industrial revenue evidence is fragmented by design: product data sits in a catalog, demand arrives through RFQ channels, supplier evidence lives in sourcing workflows, quotes move through CRM or documents, and paid outcomes belong to finance.

ELVN Revenue Knowledge Graph connects that evidence in a reproducible projection without replacing the systems that own it. Every relationship carries provenance, deterministic identity rules protect exact MPNs, and AI remains optional and human-reviewed.

The result is operational: quote-control queues, attribution paths, supplier evidence, product-enrichment priorities, margin explanations, and exception investigation.

Available through selected Design Partner and private implementation engagements.

## Six services

### 1. Revenue Graph Readiness Assessment

Source inventory, identity risks, event coverage, security boundary, use-case prioritization, and implementation roadmap. Typical one-time range: $5,000–$15,000.

### 2. Design Partner Pilot

One representative revenue path, source adapter, shadow projection, reconciliation, and two operational views. Typical one-time range: $20,000–$50,000.

### 3. Quote Control Tower

Evidence-backed queues for missing supplier responses, expired cost, margin review, approvals, customer action, and SLA risk.

### 4. Revenue Attribution Architecture

Connect canonical product pages and acquisition evidence to qualified RFQs, quotes, orders, paid invoices, and margin.

### 5. Product and MPN Intelligence

Deterministic Manufacturer + exact MPN identity, demand evidence, commercial outcomes, and catalog-enrichment prioritization.

### 6. Private Graph Implementation

Tenant-scoped deployment, RBAC, append-only events, reconciliation, backup, recovery, API boundaries, and optional governed AI.
