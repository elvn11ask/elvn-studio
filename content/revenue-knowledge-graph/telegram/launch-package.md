**ELVN Revenue Knowledge Graph — know why revenue happened.**

We built a new design-partner module for RFQ-driven industrial sales.

It connects the commercial evidence from **Product → RFQ → Supplier → Quote → Payment → Margin**, while CRM, ERP, catalog, and finance remain authoritative.

What makes it different:

• append-only event history;
• a projection that can be rebuilt and reconciled;
• provenance on every relationship;
• deterministic Manufacturer + exact MPN identity;
• currency and observed time on financial facts;
• tenant isolation and role controls;
• optional AI, disabled by default, with human review.

This is not a generic graph demo. The interfaces are task views: Quote Control Tower, revenue attribution, supplier evidence, product enrichment, margin explanation, and exception investigation.
