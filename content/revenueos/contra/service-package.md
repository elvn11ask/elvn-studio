# Contra package — ELVN Revenue Operations

## Project title

ELVN Revenue Operations — RFQ-to-Revenue Infrastructure for Industrial Distribution

## Short summary

An implementation program that connects industrial product discovery, RFQ delivery, ownership, supplier sourcing, cost, quote, order, payment, revenue, and margin across existing websites, CRM, ERP, and operational workflows.

## Full case-study description

Industrial distributors rarely operate a simple lead-to-close funnel. A buyer begins with an exact product, technical constraint, manufacturer, quantity, or BOM. The request then crosses a website, mailbox, CRM, sales queue, supplier workflow, cost sheet, quote, ERP, invoice, and payment.

ELVN Revenue Operations is the operating layer across that chain. It gives each request a durable identity, treats delivery and synchronization failures as recoverable commercial work, assigns an owner and SLA, preserves supplier and cost evidence, controls quote revisions, and connects paid outcomes back to the original product and acquisition source.

The platform is available through a Private Implementation Program. It is not presented as instant self-service SaaS. A focused pilot runs beside the existing workflow and earns production scope through reconciliation, recovery tests, and customer acceptance.

The architecture came from direct product engineering work on industrial and electronic-components catalogs where URL continuity, exact product identity, BOM/RFQ paths, performance, abuse resistance, and reversible production delivery are business requirements rather than portfolio decoration.

AI is optional. Customer-approved providers can assist with summaries, classification, drafting, and risk detection. Deterministic commercial records and human authority remain in control.

## Services offered

### RFQ Revenue Operations Audit

Fixed-scope assessment of current RFQ sources, delivery, ownership, CRM/ERP handoffs, sourcing, quotes, attribution, and operating risk.

### RevenueOS Pilot Implementation

One representative website and RFQ path, lead identity, delivery assurance, assignment, basic SLA, pipeline, dashboard, and one downstream integration.

### Industrial Catalog and RFQ Modernization

Preserve valuable public routes and technical product discovery while modernizing RFQ reliability, performance, and commercial handoffs.

### B2B Revenue Attribution

Connect canonical landing pages, products, RFQs, quotes, orders, paid invoices, and margin through a reviewable event chain.

### AI-Enabled Sales Operations Integration

Introduce customer-selected AI for bounded assistance with explicit approval, provider isolation, cost controls, and no silent commercial changes.
