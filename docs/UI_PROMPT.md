# ELVN Studio — site UI prompt

Design and implement a premium, production-ready portfolio and commercial website for ELVN Studio, an independent product engineering practice.

The website must feel positioned between a senior full-stack engineer, a focused product studio, and a practical technical consultancy. It should appeal to founders, B2B and industrial businesses, startup product teams, and clients with serious website or platform work. Do not present a fictional team, agency scale, awards, testimonials, client counts, or revenue outcomes.

Use a dark graphite visual system with off-white typography, precise neutral dividers, and one controlled acid-lime accent. Add blue, amber, or rose only to distinguish case studies. Typography should do most of the visual work: a modern grotesk with tight, editorial display headings and a mono face for evidence labels. Use generous whitespace, a 1280 px content grid, asymmetrical project layouts, restrained 20 px card radii, and subtle borders. The result should feel original and calm, not like a generic SaaS template, crypto landing page, or design-agency showreel.

Hero copy:

“I build high-performance web products that solve real business problems.”

Supporting copy:

“Full-stack development, B2B platforms, AI-ready products, technical SEO, performance engineering, and production delivery.”

Primary actions: “View selected work” and “Discuss a project.” Place real production screenshots next to the hero as a layered, restrained montage. Below it, show concise trust signals: direct senior communication, architecture before excess, measured production work, and rollback included.

Make selected work the strongest section. Feature ChipFasteners, ARMSENS Academy, and PeterhofApart or ICPROM with differentiated layouts. Every preview includes the business type, one-sentence problem, one-sentence response, exact role, selected verified evidence, relevant skills, a real screenshot, and a case-study link. Lead with business and product context before technology.

Create routes for home, work, five case studies, services, process, skills, about, resume, contact, privacy, search, and a custom 404. Case studies read like concise product-engineering reports: context, problem, constraints, solution, engineering decisions, validation, result, current state, evidence, live URL, and related work. Hide unsupported sections rather than filling them.

Model services around situations: launching a credible product or business website; fixing a slow or expensive catalog; building an MVP without immediate debt; preparing provider-independent AI capabilities; technical SEO and search architecture; production deployment and recovery. Explain who each engagement is for, what problem it solves, what is delivered, and which case study proves relevance.

Present ELVN Studio as a directly accountable independent practice covering product engineering, senior full-stack development, and technical operations. Use visible public contacts everywhere appropriate: email elvnask@gmail.com, X @elvn11ask, Telegram @elvnask, and GitHub elvn11ask. Do not render placeholder platform profiles or a personal name.

The contact page should be serious and low-friction. Group fields into about you, about the project, timing and budget, and message. Budget is optional and non-judgmental. Provide direct email, Telegram, and X alongside the form. Include clear privacy, success, validation, and failure states.

Build mobile-first and test 320, 360, 390, 430, tablet, desktop, and wide desktop layouts. Keep essential content visible, controls comfortable, screenshot text readable, and the sticky header reliable. Respect reduced motion. Use semantic HTML, logical headings, visible keyboard focus, descriptive links, accurate alt text, inline errors, and no essential hover-only content.

Keep the site lightweight: server-render static content where practical, responsive images, one font family plus mono, no autoplay video, WebGL, large animation library, carousel, background video, or client rendering for static sections. Make the site feel premium through composition, typography, evidence, and editorial discipline.

Implement unique metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots policy, OpenGraph, X cards, breadcrumbs, Person and ProfessionalService JSON-LD, project CreativeWork schema where accurate, internal links, correct 404 behavior, local build-time search, consent-first analytics preparation, and a privacy-minimized contact workflow. Do not activate analytics or verification without real values.

Writing must sound like it was written and reviewed by the studio owner. Use direct American English, concrete nouns, short claims, and transparent limitations. Never mention generated copy, assistants, prompts, or automated authorship in public-facing content.
