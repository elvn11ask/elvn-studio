# Typical implementation timeline

## Revenue Operations Audit — 1–3 weeks

Interviews, workflow and loss-point map, integration inventory, data and responsibility boundaries, baseline evidence, target architecture, commercial range, and fixed-scope roadmap.

## Focused pilot — commonly 6–12 weeks after discovery

One website and RFQ source, stable lead identity, delivery evidence, assignment and basic SLA, one downstream integration, operational dashboard, reconciliation, backup, rollback, and acceptance.

## Professional — approximately 3–6 months

Several RFQ paths, supplier and quote operations, revenue attribution, dashboards, APIs, controlled migration, training, and operational support.

## Business and enterprise — phased after discovery

Multiple teams or companies, CRM/ERP, roles, SSO, warehouse, private cloud or self-hosting, advanced audit, migration, security review, customer acceptance, and contracted SLA.

Calendar ranges assume timely customer access, decisions, test data, business owners, and integration credentials. Delay in those inputs changes the schedule.
