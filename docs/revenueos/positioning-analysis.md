# RevenueOS positioning analysis

## Recommended public name

**ELVN Revenue Operations**

Use **RevenueOS AI** only as a working or secondary product name until legal clearance. Current web research found multiple active products and services using RevenueOS, including `revenueos.de`, `revenueos.app`, and `therevenueos.com`. The unqualified name is therefore crowded and creates avoidable search and legal ambiguity.

Recommended descriptor:

**Revenue Operations Platform for Industrial Distribution**

Recommended tagline:

**Track every RFQ from product page to quote, order, revenue, and margin.**

## Positioning statement

For industrial distributors and RFQ-driven B2B teams whose commercial work is split across catalogs, inboxes, CRM, ERP, supplier communication, and spreadsheets, ELVN Revenue Operations is an RFQ and quote operating layer that makes delivery, ownership, sourcing, cost, quote, payment, and margin auditable. It integrates above existing systems and can replace fragmented spreadsheet/email workflows without pretending to replace every CRM or ERP.

## Product maturity

Public label: **Private Implementation Program**.

Evidence supports a locally validated production candidate with completed core domain implementation and integration tests. It does not yet support a claim of generally available standalone SaaS because production storefront hooks, credentials, shadow reconciliation, backup/restore rehearsal, and full operating handover have not been completed.

## Differentiation

- RFQ delivery and mailbox evidence, not only form conversion.
- industrial stage model from request through supplier, cost, quote, payment, and margin.
- deterministic SKU/MPN identity.
- SEO and product-page attribution to paid commercial outcome.
- integration layer above mixed legacy and modern systems.
- optional AI with explicit human control and customer-selected providers.
- private-cloud and self-hosted delivery without a cheap unsupported perpetual license.

## Naming recommendation

| Candidate | Distinctiveness | Clarity | Search competition | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RevenueOS AI | Low | Medium | High | Multiple active uses | Working name only |
| ELVN RevenueOS | Medium | Medium | Medium | Retains crowded core term | Secondary shorthand |
| RFQ Revenue OS | Medium | High | Lower | Descriptive and weakly ownable | Descriptor, not primary brand |
| ELVN Revenue Operations | High | High | Lower | Requires formal legal search | Recommended public name |
| ELVN RFQ Operations | High | High | Lower | Narrower future scope | Strong fallback |

This is a commercial naming recommendation, not a trademark clearance or legal opinion. No registration is claimed.
