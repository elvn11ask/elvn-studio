# Private Implementation Program offer

## Best-fit design partner

- Industrial distributor, electronic-component distributor, automation or MRO supplier, OEM sales organization, or technical B2B wholesaler.
- RFQ-driven commercial process with a real website, mailbox, CRM, ERP, supplier, quote, or attribution gap.
- A representative workflow and accountable owner are available for discovery and acceptance.
- Budget starts at the published audit or pilot range.
- The organization accepts a phased pilot and source reconciliation rather than a risky big-bang replacement.

## Offer

Start with a Revenue Operations Audit or one-path pilot. ELVN Studio documents the current event chain, defines the smallest useful operating layer, implements controlled integrations, runs shadow reconciliation, and hands over an accepted production scope with runbooks and rollback.

## Disqualification

- Requirement for instant self-service signup.
- Budget below the published discovery range.
- No access to a source workflow or business owner.
- Request to automate pricing, compliance, supplier choice, or customer communication without explicit controls.
- Expectation that AI will repair missing process ownership or unreliable source data.
- Cheap unsupported perpetual self-hosted license.
- Mandatory replacement of working ERP or CRM without evidence.
- No willingness to test backup, recovery, reconciliation, or rollback.
