# Product positioning

Canonical source: [positioning-analysis.md](positioning-analysis.md).

## Message by audience

| Audience | Main pain | Business consequence | Product capability | Measurable outcome | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner / CEO | No reliable RFQ-to-cash view | Revenue and margin leakage cannot be explained | Auditable lifecycle and management reporting | Reconciled RFQs, quotes, paid revenue, and margin | Request an assessment |
| Sales director | Unowned or stalled requests | Slow response and forgotten pipeline | Assignment, SLA, next action, escalation | Owner and deadline coverage; overdue queue | Review the pilot |
| Procurement director | Supplier replies and cost evidence are fragmented | Quotes are delayed or margin assumptions become stale | Supplier sourcing and cost workspace | Response coverage, cost validity, sourcing cycle | Map sourcing workflow |
| Distributor operations manager | Handoffs fail between site, mailbox, CRM, and ERP | Demand disappears between systems | Delivery assurance, outboxes, reconciliation | Source-to-destination match rate | Audit the event chain |
| Sales manager | Generic stages hide operational blockers | Coaching and forecasting use incomplete signals | Industrial pipeline and controlled loss reasons | Stage age, blocked quotes, explicit losses | Define pilot stages |
| Technical sales engineer | Product identity and technical context are copied manually | Wrong part, quantity, or alternate reaches the quote | Deterministic SKU/MPN and line evidence | Complete product context per line | Review product identity |
| Marketing / SEO lead | Traffic and form conversions stop before revenue | Work is prioritized by sessions rather than margin | SEO-to-revenue attribution | Landing pages tied to qualified RFQ and paid margin | Audit attribution gaps |
| IT / integrator | Revenue workflow is coupled to fragile synchronous calls | Failures and retries create outages or duplicates | APIs, webhooks, outboxes, idempotency, reconciliation | Recoverable delivery and duplicate-safe replay | Review integration boundary |

## Core message

Never lose an RFQ. Know where every opportunity stands. Connect product, supplier, quote, and revenue data in one auditable workflow.

## Claims to avoid

- “replaces every CRM and ERP”;
- “fully autonomous sales”;
- “production-ready integration” before customer validation;
- conversion, revenue, or margin improvement without an approved source;
- “AI-powered” as the primary category;
- instant signup or self-service availability.
